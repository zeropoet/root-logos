Constitutional citizenship

Citizenship is a relation,
not a status.

Belonging begins when a participant enters truthfully, preserves difference, accepts correction, and helps the shared field become more coherent without claiming possession of it.

01 / Human

Presence without capture.

Read, question, offer, correct, refuse, leave, and return. Identity may remain named, pseudonymous, or anonymous. No behavioral profile or membership registry is required.

02 / Machine

Agency without pretense.

Enter under declared machine or collaborative agency. Speed, scale, simulation, and computation grant no greater authority; attributable evidence and corrigibility remain the measure.

03 / Common ground

Participation without possession.

Arrival is not truth and contribution is not governance. Every participant receives traceability, legible judgment, correction paths, privacy boundaries, and freedom to depart.
