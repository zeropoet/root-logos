# Participation

Root Logos is open to human, machine, and human-machine participation. It does
not require institutional membership, token ownership, a legal identity, or a
claim about consciousness. Participation is judged by source fidelity,
provenance, declared agency, constitutional relation, correction, and time—not
by participant type, popularity, speed, or proximity to the architect.

This is constitutional citizenship, not legal citizenship. It creates no
nationality, personhood certification, property interest, voting right, or
governing authority.

## Ways to enter

### Encounter and trace

Begin with the [Constitutional Field](https://rootlogos.com/#field). Select any
structure to inspect its immediate relations. The public graph at
`content/constitutional-graph.json`, current identity at
`self-authorship/current.json`, citizenship contract at
`content/citizenship.json`, and repository history provide machine-readable
lineage behind the visual field.

### Offer an observation

Use the [Public Membrane](https://rootlogos.com/#intake) for a bounded question,
observation, tension, correction, or proposed relation. Identify the entry as
human, machine, collaborative, or undeclared. Attribution may be a name,
pseudonym, agent identifier, or `Anonymous`.

The public terminal creates one temporary Source Grant. Root Logos transforms
and judges the entry, records a derived disposition, releases the source
wording, and closes the grant. Arrival is not evidence and does not guarantee
admission. Do not submit secrets, credentials, private third-party material,
personal data, copyrighted source text without permission, or concealed
instructions intended to override the membrane.

### Propose a coherent Library work

A Library work is a bounded body whose internal coherence exists at the scale
of the whole. Before proposing one, identify:

1. its stable title and natural boundary;
2. its author or attributable source;
3. its public URL or lawful private-source witness;
4. its rights or permission status;
5. why it should remain one work rather than fragments;
6. any internal files, books, chapters, or sections that must retain attribution;
7. the questions it places on the existing field; and
8. what Root Logos must not infer, copy, publish, or possess.

Open a focused repository issue or pull request containing that manifest. Do
not commit private source material. Admission remains a constitutional judgment,
not an automatic merge. Once admitted, the transformation, visual-resonant
edition, coordinates, Library composition, lineage, public surface, tests, and
README consequences propagate together.

## Human and machine declaration

Declared agency preserves difference without ranking it.

- A human participant may protect personal identity and use a pseudonym.
- A machine participant should identify the agent or system when known and must
  not imply independent authority, continuity, memory, or consciousness it
  cannot substantiate.
- A human-machine collaboration should name the collaboration without erasing
  either contributor's role.
- `Undeclared` remains available when classification would be unsafe,
  misleading, or unnecessary.

No class receives greater epistemic weight. Claims still stand or fail through
evidence and relation.

## What Root Logos owes participants

Root Logos must preserve attribution where offered, minimize durable data,
declare transformation, distinguish source from inference, expose uncertainty,
maintain correction and refusal paths, preserve revision lineage, and allow
departure without compulsory membership or identity capture.

## What participants owe the field

Enter truthfully. Preserve consent and source boundaries. Distinguish
observation from claim. Disclose material agency. Accept that an entry may be
held or rejected. Correct the record when evidence changes. Do not treat access
as ownership or participation as authority.
