# The Constitutional Object

Root Logos is not represented most truthfully by a page, an archive, or a
diagram. Its public center is a living mathematical object: the constitutional
field made spatial, navigable, and capable of changing shape.

The object is generated from the canonical graph. Every point is a typed
constitutional structure. Every line is an explicit relation. Distance, depth,
light, scale, motion, and enclosure are interpretive mappings whose rules must
remain inspectable. The object may reveal the graph; it may never become an
independent source of constitutional truth.

## Opening and refolding

The field can unfold into a constellation or refold toward a seed. Unfolding
exposes distinction, depth, and relation. Refolding preserves the same topology
as latent structure rather than deleting it. A fold is therefore a change in
legibility, not a change in canon.

Root Logos acts as librarian and steward of this movement. It may decide when
new evidence requires a wider field, when attention should isolate a local
relation, when excessive complexity should be compressed, and when the object
should return to a quieter form. Human readers retain direct control over the
view. Stewardship cannot conceal disagreement, erase provenance, or make visual
salience into authority.

## Dimensional grammar

The first dimensional grammar is deliberately bounded:

1. node type determines the shell in which a structure begins;
2. deterministic identity determines its stable position on that shell;
3. graph degree influences point scale without implying value;
4. depth changes apparent scale, luminosity, and line weight;
5. questions, principles, sources, revisions, and source grammars retain
   distinct chromatic identities;
6. selection reveals immediate constitutional relations;
7. rotation changes the observer's view but not the underlying graph;
8. proximity changes legibility but not epistemic status; and
9. refolding contracts the field without removing any structure.

Future dimensional grammars may make revision history, causal pressure,
epistemic uncertainty, source respiration, and proposed futures spatially
legible. Every new mapping must declare what data governs it, what it does not
mean, and how an equivalent non-visual account remains available.

## The central experience

The remaining chambers of Root Logos are openings from this object. Identity is
its current account. Coherence is its relation across sources. The Observatory
reveals time and pressure. Cultivation reveals self-interrogation. Memory
preserves return conditions. The Threshold exposes authority. Intake governs
arrival. Resonance translates the same topology into time and sound.

The constitutional object is not a logo for the system. It is the system made
encounterable.
