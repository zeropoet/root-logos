# Root Logos 2.0 — The Weave

The Weave is Root Logos's constitutional studio operating layer. It makes the architecture revealed across the Zeropoet studio inspectable and maintainable without absorbing the authority of its sources.

Its primary public surface is intentionally non-discursive. The field does not display the constitution as doctrine or narrate a mythology. The constitution acts underneath as behavior, boundary, and machine-readable law. Names and roles appear only after deliberate inspection. Agents enter through `/agent.json`; humans and machines encounter the same field through different readable surfaces.

The repositories were not designed from a complete plan. They separated while individual works discovered their identities, laws, and responsibilities. Together they revealed a latent architecture. Their fragmentation was diagnostic and generative; it is evidence, not destiny.

Root Logos 2.0 uses that evidence to derive a stronger structure:

- canonical facts remain with the project that owns their meaning;
- relations carry an explicit kind, status, evidence basis, and authority boundary;
- shared infrastructure emerges only where a shared law has been demonstrated;
- resilient copies identify their origin and synchronization policy;
- agents receive narrow, inspectable stewardship rather than implied authority;
- refactoring preserves provenance, continuity, refusal, and local identity.

The governing rule is:

> Do not preserve historical separation merely because it exists, and do not unify what has not demonstrated a shared law.

## Foundation

- `constitution.json` is the machine-readable Weaving constitution.
- `system.json` is the provisional studio-wide node and relation registry.
- `sources/` holds bounded source declarations or attributed snapshots.
- `schemas/` defines the structural contracts Root Logos can validate.
- `drift.json` records unresolved cross-project pressure without silently selecting a winner.

This layer begins additively. Root Logos 1.6 remains the operational public experience while the 2.0 architecture proves its mappings and migration boundaries.

## One object, several projections

Telos, ØVEL, and Root Logos provide the three abstracted index experiences through which the emerging whole can be built and encountered. They are not competing centers and are not interchangeable applications:

- **ØVEL** exposes the object's perceptual physics.
- **Telos** exposes its declared relations, memory, and keeping function.
- **Root Logos** exposes its conditions for participation, correction, and emergence.
- **The Record** makes the object's distributed originating voices available to hearing.

Together they illuminate one living object through different authority-preserving projections. No projection owns the whole. A transformation in one may exert bounded pressure on the others through declared data relations, but it cannot silently rewrite their local truth.
